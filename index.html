Everything you need in one place

Designed for creators who want multi-platform reach and local Tanzanian payments without the complexity.

📡

Multi-Platform Streaming

Push one stream to TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, Twitch, Kick and custom RTMP at the same time. Vertical + landscape support.

💬

Unified Comments

See comments from all platforms in one feed. Quick reply, pin important messages, and set auto-replies for common questions.

💰

M-Pesa & Mobile Money

Receive tips and donations directly to Vodacom M-Pesa, Yas (Tigo), Airtel Money, Halotel, TTCL and Azania Bank accounts.

⚙️

Per-Platform Settings

Different titles, descriptions, privacy, resolution and bitrate for each platform. Save presets for quick go-live.

Server or Local

Keys Stay Yours

Stream keys and payment credentials are stored only on your side. No cloud middleman holds your platform logins.

How to go live

Four clear steps. No complicated setup required.

1

Connect platforms

Add stream keys or OAuth for YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and others.

2

Set payments

Enter your M-Pesa / Yas / Airtel number and create tip messages or payment links.

3

Choose source

Stream from OBS on your PC or from a small VPS restreamer server.

4

Go live

Hit start. Monitor comments and tips from this same dashboard.

Ready to stream across Africa?

This is a ready-to-use design and structure. Host it free on GitHub Pages. For real multi-streaming you connect a VPS restreamer or OBS. For real M-Pesa collection use IntaSend, PlusPesa or similar.
